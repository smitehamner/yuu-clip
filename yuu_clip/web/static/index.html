Speaker labels

Identifies who is speaking within each audio track and prefixes transcripts accordingly
Speaker model
SpeechBrain is included with YuuClip - no HuggingFace account or token needed. The speaker model (~80 MB) downloads automatically the first time you run Detect Speakers.
How readily two moments of speech are treated as different people within one recording. Lower splits into more speakers; higher merges more together. 0.85 is the default. If one person keeps getting split into many speakers, raise it; if two people are being merged, lower it.
A voice that speaks for fewer than this many seconds in a recording is treated as background noise or crosstalk and folded into the nearest speaker. Raise it if you still get spurious extra speakers; lower it (or set 0 to turn it off) to keep every brief voice. 10 seconds is the default.
How similar a voice must be to re-attach to an already-named speaker on Re-detect Speakers. Higher is stricter - fewer wrong matches, but more voices re-listed as new "Speaker N" to re-confirm. 0.75 is a safe default; lower it if named speakers keep coming back unnamed.
How sure the app must be that a voice in a new recording is the same person you already named in another one. Higher = fewer wrong matches. Matches are only ever suggested - you confirm them in People, nothing is merged automatically. 0.80 is a safe default.

Hot-words

Boost a clip's score when a phrase is spoken - great for running gags or catchphrases. Leave one Global to apply everywhere, or scope it to a world context so it only affects recordings tagged with that context. Saved instantly; use a recording's Additional Actions → Rescan Hot-words (also offered right after saving, as a toast shortcut) to apply changes to its existing clips without a full re-score.

Sensitive Content

Flag clips containing names, personal info, or language you want to catch before sharing - kept completely separate from scoring, so it never changes a clip's score, only warns. Privacy Terms are names or personal info you don't want in shared clips; Censor Words are language to flag before posting to restricted platforms. Leave one Global to flag everywhere, or scope it to a world context to flag only recordings tagged with that context. Saved instantly and applied to every clip right away.

      Getting Started

      Checking your setup…
      Your workflow
      1. Analyze - click + New Recording and select a recording. YuuClip transcribes the audio, detects scene changes, and scores every clip.
      2. Review - step through the clip list. Watch the preview, read the description, and press A to approve, R to reject, or U to mark it unreviewed again.
      3. Export - click Export on any clip to extract it. Quick export copies the video out in seconds; Precise re-encodes for frame-accurate cuts. The export window tells you which one you'll get.
      4. Build a reel - click Highlight Reels in the header, then Build Reel to compile approved clips into a single highlight reel with title cards and transitions.
      What the scores mean
      ⭐ OverallWeighted blend of the four sub-scores
      😂 FunnyJokes, absurdist moments, chaotic banter
      🎭 DramaticConfrontations, revelations, emotional beats
      ⚔️ ActionCombat, tension, high-stakes moments
      🎬 VisualOn-screen intensity - scene cuts and motion, even in silence
      Key concepts
      Track layoutMaps your OBS audio tracks to roles (player voice, game sounds, etc.) so YuuClip knows what to transcribe
      World contextTells the LLM scorer who's in your recordings and what the setting is - improves descriptions and scores for your specific game
      Clip vs SceneClips are punchy 15-90 second moments; scenes are longer 1-5 minute stretches with a story arc. If you turn on Generate scenes, they share the clip list with a SCENE badge and the All / Clips / Scenes buttons filter between them
      Quick tips
      • Use the search box and score filter in the sidebar to find specific moments
      • Press ← / → to move between clips without touching the mouse
      • Open Controls from the menu for the full keyboard shortcut list
      • Click World Contexts in the top bar to add people and setting info before scoring
