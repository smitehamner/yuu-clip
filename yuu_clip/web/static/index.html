      Getting Started

      Your workflow
      1. Analyze — click + Analyze and select a recording. yuu-clip transcribes the audio, detects scene changes, and scores every clip.
      2. Review — step through the clip list. Watch the preview, read the description, and press A to approve or R to reject.
      3. Export — click Export on any clip to extract it. Quick export copies the video out in seconds; Precise re-encodes for frame-accurate cuts. The export window tells you which one you'll get.
      4. Build a reel — click Highlight Reels in the header, then Build Reel to compile approved clips into a single highlight reel with title cards and transitions.
      What the scores mean
      ⭐ OverallWeighted average of the three sub-scores
      😂 FunnyJokes, absurdist moments, chaotic banter
      🎭 DramaticConfrontations, revelations, emotional beats
      ⚔️ ActionCombat, tension, high-stakes moments
      Key concepts
      Track layoutMaps your OBS audio tracks to roles (player voice, game sounds, etc.) so yuu-clip knows what to transcribe
      World contextTells the LLM scorer who your characters are and what the setting is — improves descriptions and scores for your specific game
      Quick tips
      • Use the search box and score filter in the sidebar to find specific moments
      • Press ← / → to move between clips without touching the mouse
      • Open Controls from the menu for the full keyboard shortcut list
      • Open World Contexts from the menu to add character and setting info before scoring
